How it works

From camera to advice in four steps.

  1. Capture

    The camera watches your lettuce bed and sends live images to the app.

  2. Detect

    The machine learning model finds each plant and classifies its condition.

  3. Measure

    The soil moisture sensor reports the moisture level on a simple gauge.

  4. Act

    The app records the result in your logs and shows organic treatment steps for any detected disease.

Powdery mildew

Moderate risk

Symptoms and overview

Fungal condition showing white powdery spots on leaves. Spores thrive in high humidity and poor airflow.

Immediate organic actions

  • Prune heavily infected lower leaves and dispose of them outside the garden.
  • Spray leaves with cold-pressed organic neem oil (1 tsp neem + 1/2 tsp organic soap per liter of water).
  • Apply an organic milk spray (1 part raw or diluted milk to 9 parts water) under morning sunlight.
  • Increase plant spacing to 8–10 inches for fresh air circulation.
